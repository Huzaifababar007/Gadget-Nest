# Screenshots Guide for GadgetNest

## 📸 How to Add Screenshots

### 1. Take Screenshots
Visit [gadgetnests.netlify.app](https://gadgetnests.netlify.app) and take screenshots of:

1. **Desktop Homepage** - Full homepage on desktop
2. **Mobile Homepage** - Homepage on mobile device
3. **Product Catalog** - Products page
4. **Shopping Cart** - Cart with items
5. **Product Details** - Individual product page
6. **Category Page** - Products filtered by category

### 2. Upload to Imgur
1. Go to [imgur.com](https://imgur.com)
2. Upload your screenshots
3. Copy the direct image URLs

### 3. Update README.md
Replace the placeholder URLs in the README.md with your actual Imgur URLs:

```markdown
### Desktop View
![GadgetNest Desktop](YOUR_IMGUR_URL_HERE)

### Mobile View
![GadgetNest Mobile](YOUR_IMGUR_URL_HERE)

### Product Catalog
![GadgetNest Products](YOUR_IMGUR_URL_HERE)

### Shopping Cart
![GadgetNest Cart](YOUR_IMGUR_URL_HERE)
```

### 4. Recommended Screenshot Sizes
- **Desktop**: 1920x1080 or 1440x900
- **Mobile**: 375x667 (iPhone) or 360x640 (Android)
- **Tablet**: 768x1024

### 5. Screenshot Tips
- Use browser dev tools to simulate different screen sizes
- Take screenshots in different sections of the site
- Show the responsive design
- Highlight key features like cart, search, categories
